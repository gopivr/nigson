## Summary

Implemented the Commerce-owned negotiated quote aggregate and API-195 dependency. Quote drafts/revisions, separate FINANCE approval, offer, customer read/acceptance, immutable revisions and event/audit effects execute in Commerce transactions. Experience lead lookup/association stays behind private APIs 130/131; Commerce does not query Experience tables. API-195 derives a temporary private cart from the approved frozen quote revision, obtains a FedEx rate outside a database transaction, then revalidates and binds the quote revision, destination, package snapshot, mode and trusted account fingerprint. Checkout verifies the private cart’s complete frozen SKU/quantity set against the approved offer, preserves negotiated amounts, and rejects ordinary cart APIs against quote carts. A DB trigger prevents source quote revision rotation. Public carrier DTOs are unchanged.
