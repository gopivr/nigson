## Summary

Implemented the Commerce-owned customer reward ledger and referral flows for API-062, API-063, API-064, and API-161. Available balances are derived from append-only, currency-scaled entries with availability and expiry windows. Referral identifiers are high-entropy random codes, persisted per party, and only produce links from validated HTTPS configuration. Attribution checks current identity, active parties, self-referral, prior settled purchase, one-attribution uniqueness, and exactly one enabled NG referral rule before persisting.

Verified successful settlement facts to append idempotent rule-based reward earns; scheduled qualification is a transactional pending-to-qualified transition. Settled refunds append proportional cumulative reversals (or full-on-any-refund where configured), capped at the original earning, with durable business keys and audit. Reward writes are wired into Commerce order settlement and refund settlement transactions; provider calls remain outside database locks. Public DTOs are documented in the generated OpenAPI contract. The additive `available_at` migration preserves existing schema history.
