# ADR-031: Basic affiliate applications

20 November 2026. User authorized a minimal staged affiliate implementation.

First increment reuses existing typed Experience partner applications, Commerce entitlement activation, pending/ACK recovery, Admin Partner Applications and profile status. AFFILIATE has no business documents, business reference, bank fields or price assignment. Signed-in customers submit contact/channel details; current Sales-authorized staff approve/reject with a reason or suspend an approved affiliate. Direct affiliate approval does not require the independent business-document reviewer. Existing WHOLESALE/DISTRIBUTOR review safeguards remain unchanged.

Commerce records AFFILIATE_MARKETING separately from configured BUSINESS_PURCHASING. The existing organization record is only an owner-local account grouping for this acknowledged access; it grants neither staff roles nor wholesale/distributor purchasing. Retained affiliate/business claims are mutually exclusive under a party lock, even after suspension. Duplicate affiliate activation from another application is denied. Existing routes/workload scopes are reused; no cross-owner SQL. Approved/suspended UI status follows Commerce ACK, never optimistic success. Existing leads are not converted into approved applications.

Future agreed policy: 5% retail merchandise after discounts excluding shipping/tax; last valid same-browser referral within seven days; no self-referrals; available seven days after delivery; monthly manual full-available-balance payouts after reviewed bank details. This increment does not implement links, financial accrual, payout storage or money transfers. Existing customer referral rewards remain separate.

Additive Commerce048 and Experience044 expand partner-type checks only. Applied migrations remain immutable; rollback refuses existing affiliate rows. Required tests cover direct approval/no documents, bank-field rejection, approval/replay/suspension, retail price isolation, exclusive partner claims/races and existing business review regression.

Second increment (AFFILIATE-LINKS-001): approved affiliates create stable random product links from Profile > Affiliate product links. Public same-origin links redirect to the stored product UUID and set a separate HttpOnly SameSite=Lax seven-day visit cookie, Secure outside explicitly insecure local. The existing login return route preserves the product page; no referral screen or identity cookie changes. Last valid link replaces the browser visit attribution. Commerce checks current affiliate access, expiry, self-referral and retail context when freezing checkout attribution. Existing order commercial snapshots retain the selected affiliate even after later clicks. Separate additive Commerce049/050 own link/visit persistence; bounded expired-visit cleanup and public request budget apply. No commission accrual, bank collection or payout is implemented by this increment.

Third increment (AFFILIATE-COMMISSIONS-001): new checkout attribution freezes commissionRate=0.05 and holdDays=7. Verified local payment settlement appends one immutable NGN earning on merchandise subtotal less discounts, excluding shipping/tax. Existing RewardOperations is composed with the affiliate engine so order/refund facts and entries/audit commit together; no external calls or duplicate payment handling path. Old orders without the frozen policy are not retroactively commissioned. User confirmed proportional partial refund reversals: cumulative successful refund-line merchandise excluding tax determines the rounded cumulative 5% reversal, less prior reversals, capped at original earning. Uncertain/pending refunds hold the balance, never reverse on an unverified outcome. Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks do not duplicate entries.

API-225 and the existing account Affiliate page show only the current affiliate's earnings. Available means all-order confirmed delivery plus seven days, no active undelivered shipments, and no refund/operational hold. Delivery timing and balances are derived from persisted facts at read time, with bounded pages and repeatable-read totals, avoiding a new scheduler or editable balance counter. Suspended affiliates may inspect historical earnings. No buyer details or other customers' order pages are exposed. Commerce051 adds the append-only earning/reversal table with runtime SELECT/INSERT only; rollback refuses populated data. Bank details, payout reviews and monthly manual bulk payout records remain the next increment.

Fourth increment (AFFILIATE-PAYOUTS-001): user confirmed normal-login ADMIN/FINANCE banking review and manual payout recording, and recovery of post-paid refund reversals from future earnings. After affiliate approval, collect Nigerian bank name, holder name and ten-digit account number on the existing Affiliate page; application forms remain unchanged. Bank data uses the existing Commerce AES-GCM payment keys with bank-specific party/row context and secret random padding before the content commitment. No new keys/config or provider is invented. Preserve historical decryption keys during existing payment-key rotation; bank retention/reprotection must be considered before retiring them. Own/list reads mask numbers; explicit staff detail read is audited and no-store. Bank changes create a new PENDING revision and invalidate the previous review. PREPARED payouts lock bank edits.

API-226..233 implement own overview, encrypted bank revision save, authorized affiliate list/detail, review, prepare, paid recording and no-transfer cancellation. Preparation reserves the complete net available NGN balance using current reviewed bank details; one prepared payout globally per affiliate, one prepared/paid payout per Africa/Lagos month. Immutable paid/prepared totals deduct from qualified commission sums; later refunds/holds offset future qualified earnings, without rewriting or deleting paid truth. Preparation and commission changes share the beneficiary party lock. Transfer references serialize through a scoped advisory lock and are globally unique. No money is transferred by the application. Record PAID only after external bank confirmation; preserve PREPARED if outcome is uncertain. Cancellation requires explicit confirmation that no transfer started. If eligibility changes after preparation, the screen blocks starting a new transfer, but staff may still record an already-confirmed bank transfer truth and recover the difference later.

Commerce052/053 add bank revisions/payout state; Commerce054 enforces immutable bank payloads and payout amount/bank/month snapshots, valid review/terminal transitions and retained history. Populated downgrade refuses. New audit actions/resources remain bank-number-free. Both current persisted ADMIN and FINANCE can use these endpoints without recent MFA, per the user's explicit decision; other privileged workflows retain their existing MFA rules. Account payouts and Admin > Affiliate Payouts reuse existing shells; Roles & Permissions reflects the new page grant. No withdrawal requests, automatic monthly scheduler, automatic bank verification, transfer API/provider, CSV exports or notification subsystem is added.
Durable command clarification: preparation Idempotency-Key is the permanent payout UUID; compare original actor/beneficiary/bank on replay, preserving the original resource even after temporary command-record expiry and month changes. Bank-save request UUID maps one-way with party/purpose to a distinct opaque revision UUID; public masked bank IDs are not replay keys or a plaintext-guessing oracle.
